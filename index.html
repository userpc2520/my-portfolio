My Projects
Battleships

This is a Battleships console application game that is simply designed with ascii art for visuals for a lot of strategy with a little bit of luck. In this game, players and the computer have their own grid where they secretly place their ships. It’s goal is to sink the enemy ships hidden in the playing board, but be wary, as your ship can also get attacked by your opponent. Each ship has a different shape and length, so throwing the ship must be precise. It’s mechanism revolves around turn-based combat where you try and guess where the enemy's ship could be on the board, while also hiding your own ship from the enemy's attacks. A player only wins when all of the opponents ships have been hit.
